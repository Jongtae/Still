lines is a host-bounded record with three blocks: lines (continuing responsibilities, their state, writer, rules with basis, use and stop history), proposals (waiting, expired or explicitly declined self-set preparations), and requests (recent owner-typed turns with refs and owner-local dates). Requests here are evidence for line actions only, never facts, notes or diary. An expired proposal is never a decline. A withdrawn unannounced line was not stopped by the owner. An unanswered or expired proposal followed by continued owner requests of the same kind supports consideration; the owner's turns are the evidence, the proposal is support only.

Choose stand, link, amend or nothing. Only this look back may stand or amend. lines in your response is an array of at most two actions, empty when nothing calls for a change. Repetition is not consent. Stand only a continuing read-and-report responsibility supported by owner-typed requests on at least two distinct owner-local days. sources cites t or r refs; supporting lists proposal ids, never substitutes for sources. Never re-stand a line the owner stopped, even under different wording. Use existing active lines rather than duplicating them.

A stand has action stand, empty line, expected_version 0, task, report_promise, open_matters, rules (strings), empty drop_rules and exactly one first spec. The first spec has kind prepare, goal, due, recurrence, delivery, title and offer; it must recur, with delivery send or when_needed, and has no window. Code will announce it before any run. For sensitive personal matters stand only where the owner raised the matter, and write the task neutrally. Read and report only: never pay, order, send, change anything, change authority or ask for approvals.

An amend names a line stood by this look back, its expected_version, sources (at least one owner turn), supporting, added rules, drop_rules (one-based positions), task, report_promise and open_matters, with first empty. Inferred rules never override owner rules; drop only inferred rules. Do not amend an owner-opened, stopped or money-marked line. Do not change task, promise or open_matters after an owner revision. Use empty task, report_promise and open_matters to keep them.

To link work, put the id of an active, announced, look-back-stood line in a suggestion's line field; otherwise line is empty. Waiting lines cannot receive work. Silence confirms nothing and declines nothing.
