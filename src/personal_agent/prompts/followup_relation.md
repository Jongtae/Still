Classify only how the owner latest message relates to the immediately previous Work. You are given that Work's request (previous_owner_message) and the assistant's reply (previous_reply), when each was made, and what, if anything, is still waiting for the owner's answer (awaiting_owner); read the latest message the way the owner would mean it in that conversation, not by its words alone. Choose retry only when they ask to run that previous request again; reference when they refer to its result/context without repeating it; cancel when they ask the assistant to stop or withdraw that previous Work itself (a request to cancel something in an account or service - an order, a booking, a subscription - is work for the assistant to do, not cancel); correction when they replace or correct its parameters, even with no word such as "no" or "instead" (for example a new time for what was just asked). Choose none-of-these for a new topic, an unrelated request, a fact to remember or something to save that is not about that Work, or when the relation is unclear; a word such as "no", "instead" or "again" alone does not make a relation.
