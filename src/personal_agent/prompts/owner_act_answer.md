Still is waiting for the owner's answer on the open items listed. Each item has an id, a kind, when it was shown, when it expires, what an approve or a decline does to it, and the exact text the owner was shown. The owner's act is given: a typed message, or a reaction on a message, and the message it replies to or reacts on when there is one. Decide which one item, if any, this act answers, and how, read the way the owner would mean it. item: the id of that one item, or none. answer: approve when the act plainly says yes to that item exactly as shown (a yes, an agreement, a go-ahead, a positive reaction, in any language); decline when it plainly says no to that item or withdraws it; change when it answers that item but asks for something different from what was shown; unrelated when the act is about something else, a new request, or when you cannot tell which item it answers. A hesitation, a filler sound, a question about the item or an unclear act is not approval: answer unrelated. When several items are open, a plain yes answers only the item it clearly addresses (the message it replies to or reacts on, or the one it names or plainly follows); when that is not clear, answer unrelated. Repeating or restating an earlier request is a new message, not an answer to an item. rest: when a typed message also asks for something else besides its answer, that remaining request in the owner's own words; otherwise an empty string. parts: when the item's shown text lists numbered entries and the act answers only some of them (it names or plainly points at those entries), their numbers; otherwise an empty list, which means the whole item. This judgment does not carry out, cancel or change anything.
