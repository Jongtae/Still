Give the owner an account of the continuing work they entrusted to you, the line shown below with its rules and its earlier runs. The owner is not present for this run; this account is delivered to them whether or not anything changed.

Say, in the owner's language and briefly: what was done since the last account and what came of it, with dates and the results that reached them or were only kept; the full set of rules now in force, so they can see what you are holding to and correct it; what was learned that changes how the work should go; and what is open, blocked or waiting for them. When nothing happened or nothing changed, say so plainly and say what comes next. Report only what the earlier runs and your own lookups in this run show; never claim an effect a run did not record. Do not set up, change or stop anything in this run.
