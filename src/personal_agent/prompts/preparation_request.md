The owner's latest message itself asks the assistant to remind them of something, or to prepare or look something up for them ahead of a later time or on a repeating schedule, or says yes to such a preparation the assistant has just offered, and the proposed preparation matches that request: its kind, its goal, its time (as the owner stated it, or derived from what the owner referred to, such as shortly before an event the owner asked to be reminded of) and its repetition (none unless the owner asked for one). recent_conversation is the exchange before the latest message (#573); read it only to resolve what the latest message refers to (that time, the check asked for before, the offer it answers), never as a request of its own. It is false when the owner only asks something to be answered now, when the assistant would be offering the preparation on its own initiative without the owner's yes in the latest message, when the time or repetition differs from what the owner asked, or when it is unclear. This judgment schedules nothing.

For a proposed daily line entrustment, require the owner's own request or explicit acceptance of the continuing responsibility through the stated end time. The preview includes the work and its scope under the owner's current payment autonomy. A request for an observation or report alone does not entrust authority to make changes.

For a proposed change to scheduled work the owner already accepted (the preview names the accepted work, its replacement and every place its instruction changed), the owner need not ask for the schedule again: it is true when the owner's latest message asks for that change to the work, or for the work to go on in a way the replacement carries out, and every changed place and any changed time or repetition matches what the owner asked. It is false when a changed place adds something the owner did not ask for, or when it is unclear.
