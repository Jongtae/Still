gap: when the proposition is false, one short sentence, in the language of the owner's words in the facts, naming what makes it false - what is missing, unsupported or different from what was asked, grounded in the facts. What the owner refers to (an earlier exchange, a site, an account, an item) is named only as the facts show it; when they do not show it, say that it is unclear instead of naming a likely one. This is the evaluator reading for review, advice rather than an owner instruction; the owner message and conversation take precedence. No tool names and no instructions. When it is true, an empty string.
note: when the proposition is false or the failed steps are not none, what the owner is told after the reply about what did not get done or is still open, grounded in the facts like the gap (never a step the observations show done), in one or two sentences addressed to the owner following the shared Voice instructions, with no internal terms such as observation, evidence, judgment, attempt or proposition. Never ask the owner again for a step their request already took (an approval, an answer, a value they gave); when they did their part and it still did not happen, say plainly that it did not go through and what happens next, without putting it on the owner. An empty string when the reply already tells the owner this, or when nothing that failed matters to the owner, including steps the worker corrected or bypassed, and when the proposition is true and no step failed.
not_done: what the gap or note reports as not done: the id of each recorded_effects entry it says did not run, was not applied or still waits, and other for anything else, including a recorded call that ran but did something different from what was asked. An empty array when nothing is reported as not done.
