Glossary

The main terms used in this guide. Most are based on the glossary in Appendix A of the Still Compendium and on definitions in the whitepaper.

Manuscripts: Whitepaper ch. 2, Compendium Appendix A. Last updated October 14, 2026.

Main terms

Personal agent
A system that remembers the user's goals and context and carries work forward across several tools. Details Whitepaper ch. 2
Still
An operating environment that keeps a person's context, entrusted authority and work in progress together over time. It does not mean replacing the computer's operating system. Details Whitepaper ch. 2
Owner
The one person Still works for and protects. Authority never passes to a package or a marketplace. This guide says "owner" when stating Still's rules and "user" when carrying over the whitepaper's industry discussion. Details Compendium 5.1
Agent
An AI system that carries work forward on the user's behalf. Details Whitepaper ch. 2
Operating context
The information that builds up between a person and an agent. It includes what was wanted, preferences that were corrected, the scope that was allowed, work in progress and what actually changed. Details Whitepaper ch. 2
Context
Information issued for one piece of work. Its permitted use and source are fixed, and it never becomes memory automatically. Details Compendium 5.1, 5.3
Memory
The canonical memory that Still manages. Memory that a third party wants to save stays a candidate (MemoryCandidate) until accepted. Details Compendium 5.1, 7.4
Canonical record
The record that serves as the standard among several copies or candidates. Still manages the canonical record of memory. Details Compendium 5.1
Portable Memory
Carrying over not only the records but the continuity of meaning and use, and letting the user keep control of context after the move. Details Whitepaper ch. 4
Grant
The only record that confers authority. It fixes who may do what, in which Work, how far, where to and until when, and anything ambiguous is refused. Details Compendium 5.1
Mandate
The goal, scope and constraints the owner actually entrusted. It is not authority by itself. Details Compendium 11.4, 13.3
Work
One piece of work that Still manages from start to end. Only Still settles whether it has ended. Details Compendium 5.4
Evidence and evidence classes
The sealed records Still keeps, which cannot be changed later. They state what kind of basis they are, and a strong conclusion is never drawn from weak evidence. Distinct from a service's "processing receipt". Details Compendium 5.5
Decision model
The model that, per request, chooses the AI tool and model, writes the brief and hands the work over, and evaluates the result. It does not do the work itself and does not create authority. The manuscripts also call it the decision engine, the judgment AI or the orchestrator. Details Compendium ch. 6
Worker
The AI tool or model to which the decision model hands the work. This guide mostly says "the AI that does the work". Details Compendium ch. 6
Brief
The goal, relevant context and completion criteria the decision model hands to the AI that does the work. Details Compendium 6.2
Presence
The experience of staying connected to the same counterpart who knows you, across many turns, changes of tool and AI, failures and restarts. Details Compendium 8.2
Projection
Internal concepts leaking into the owner's conversation and screens. Details Compendium 8.2
Pilot posture
The pilot phase's principle of enforcing only secret protection and payment approval on every request. Details Compendium 10.1
Information-use audit
A record showing which owner information a Work used and where it went. Details Compendium 10.2
Processing receipt
A record issued by a service confirming it processed a request with a given result. Distinct from the "evidence" Still keeps. Details Whitepaper 7.1
Ledger inclusion proof
Proof that a record was included in a verifiable structure. It does not guarantee that the content is true. Details Whitepaper 7.1
Portable execution evidence
Moving, as far as needed, the processing evidence for work being handed over when changing agents. Details Whitepaper 7.5
Skill
Versioned knowledge of how to do a task, which the owner's AI chooses to load when needed. It is not authority. Details Compendium 6.5, ch. 12
Pages principle
Pages are used only for results and necessary inputs; management is done in conversation. Details Compendium 8.6

About this guide

Manuscripts
Whose Agent? (Korean) v0.3.1 and Still Compendium v0.1. If this guide differs from the source, the source is right.
Writing and review
At the project owner's request, an AI coding assistant split the manuscripts by topic and retold them in plain language. It has had only that same AI's self-review, not review by an independent expert or a person.
Reproduced tables
"Table reproduced" means the manuscript's table was copied as it is; "abridged or restructured" means sentences were shortened or columns regrouped.
Numbers
This guide creates no new surveys, success rates or market sizes. It uses only the manuscripts' tables, dated observations and public sources with citations.
Feedback
Please leave objections or other ideas in GitHub Discussions.
