How Still keeps continuity

The decisions behind a personal agent that outlasts its workers.

Implementation, evidence and direction are separate. Source code shows mechanisms; tests show only their exercised scope; owner recordings show selected sessions. Canonical contracts take precedence over this guide. Research does not activate implementation.

01 / Engineering philosophy

Keep the relationship outside the worker.

Implementation / evidence. Still keeps owner state while the chosen AI does the work. C15 favours reuse; C16 assigns semantic judgment to AI and exact authority, secret, budget and effect invariants to code.

Design / limits. These are engineering commitments. The five-plane architecture and distribution platform are broader than the shipped slice; package schemas do not establish a working marketplace.

02 / Ontology & Context

What is known, from whom, and for how long?

Implementation / evidence. CurrentContext and owner-model modules represent current observations and durable knowledge. The context contract distinguishes source reports, interpretations, time-limited hypotheses and canonical Memory; they are not interchangeable facts.

Design / limits. The role-ontology research explores relationships, offers, agreements and mandates. It is a concept proposal, not a universal ontology engine. Domain facts, professional lenses, mandate and enforced authority stay distinct.

03 / Decision Model

When should the assistant answer, ask or delegate?

Implementation / evidence. DecisionEngine supplies provider-neutral semantic judgments. The orchestrator chooses a worker/model and brief, evaluates the observed outcome through goal_reached and can revise within bounds. Current amendments in the decision contract override historical plan fields.

Design / limits. A judgment cannot create a Grant, verify an unobserved effect or turn unknown into success. Qualification tests exercise named routes; they do not establish live task quality for every model.

04 / Presence

One relationship, responsive while work continues.

Implementation / evidence. Conversation handoff and orchestration carry owner corrections into work and later plans. The professional-roles contract inventories delivered steering paths and remaining gaps. The product-status page keeps observed support separate from intended experience.

Design / limits. Continuity, one voice, responsiveness, memory, availability and conversational recovery are the research definition. Full foreground relevance reporting and cross-role communication remain bounded by the documented gaps; a progress animation does not prove them.

05 / Model independence

Change the engine without rebuilding the relationship.

Implementation / evidence. The AI-switch manifest records one owner-supplied Telegram session on 8 October 2026. It shows switching between Claude Code and Codex with prior conversation references. State ownership is defined independently of workers.

Design / limits. This is screenshot evidence of selected replies, not an independent account trace or a release-specific repeatability test. Models may differ in tools and behavior. Local installation still sends selected context to a configured remote AI.

06 / Owner Authority

Who may act, and what actually happened?

Implementation / evidence. The current single-owner pilot keeps folder scope, secrets, per-action payment approval, no replay and Memory authority boundaries. Its current egress policy uses a per-Work information-use audit; the Secretary contract records amendments to older gates.

Design / limits. The six owner rights are acceptance requirements, not a blanket enforcement claim. A mandate, installed skill or connected account never grants an action by itself. Requested, observed, partial, failed and unknown outcomes must remain distinct.

07 / Research & Evidence

Read the evidence at its actual scope.

Implementation / evidence. The release manifest records published-build coverage and installed smoke checks. Product status distinguishes newer main, synthetic journeys and owner recordings. The demo was edited from 11 October 2026 sessions; the archived AI-switch clip has its own provenance.

The former site clip stops after a recommendation. Its provenance records that the subsequent requested cart swap removed the old beans but failed to add the replacement and hit a limit; that failure is outside the clip.

Design / limits. Synthetic success is not live-service proof; a recorded assistant reply is not independent effect verification. Research and editorial reviews do not activate runtime features. The preserved ontology review was one model in sequential roles, not independent expert or multi-model review. Private predecessor issue links may be inaccessible; public manuscript and provenance files remain readable.
