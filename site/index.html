Get started with Still

Use Still on Mac, Linux or Windows (WSL2). Connect a ChatGPT or Claude subscription, an API key, or Ollama to get started.

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jongtae/Still/ebf97d4150be3eb867649a31e79287f05db02886/scripts/install.sh | sh

The URL pins the installer to one version; read the script before you run it. On an Apple silicon Mac with Homebrew: brew tap jongtae/still https://github.com/Jongtae/Still, brew install jongtae/still/agentos, then agentos start. On Windows, run wsl --install in PowerShell, restart, open Ubuntu and paste the command there.

  1. Connect the AI you want to use

    After installation, connect your AI in the terminal. Use a ChatGPT subscription through Codex, a Claude subscription through Claude Code, an API key, or Ollama. An AI you are already signed in to is preselected.

  2. Start a conversation with Still

    Talk to Still in the terminal, or open 127.0.0.1:8787 in your browser. Keep agentos start running in the terminal while you use Still.

  3. Chat on your phone through Telegram

    Say “connect Telegram”, paste the token @BotFather gives you into the hidden input field, and press Start in the pairing link.

    Optional: give the chat Still’s look with a wallpaper (with Still or plain): in the Telegram chat, open the menu, choose Change Wallpaper and pick it from your photos.

Everything else is in the quick start guide.

Use what you shared before to guide the next request.

Still remembers preferences and circumstances you share, and refers to them for related requests. You can check each memory’s source and undo saved memories.

Illustrative conversation

Monday

My cat has been off her food lately. What should I try?

A few things worth trying first… I’ve noted that you have a cat.

Thursday

Find me a robot vacuum under $500.

Three under $500. I put the one that handles cat hair best first.

Where you can talk to Still

  • Telegram
  • Slack
  • Terminal
  • Mattermost
  • Browser

Tools you can connect

  • Your folders
  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Drive
  • Sites you sign in to

Folders are read-only unless you say otherwise. Gmail organising is a separate permission, and Still never sends mail.

Ask questions or change your request while a task runs.

Before a longer task, Still explains its plan and which AI will do the work. You can ask another question or change direction while the task runs.

Illustrative conversation

Find a birthday gift for Mum under $50.

CodexI’ll look for something she’d use every day, check reviews and delivery dates, then show you three.

How do you say thank you in Japanese?

Arigatō gozaimasu. The gift search is still going.

  • Work stays within the permissions you set. Still follows the access scope and action permissions set for your folders, accounts and tools.
  • Every payment needs your approval. Even after an item is added to a cart, you must separately approve payment.
  • Passwords and tokens are protected. They are excluded from content sent to the AI model, logs and execution records.
